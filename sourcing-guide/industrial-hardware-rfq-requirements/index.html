Industrial Hardware RFQ Requirements

Engineering hardware RFQs usually need more context than a product name or catalog image. Current CHS source materials cover several product families beyond standard fasteners, including precision rolled threaded steel components, formwork and temporary-works hardware, anchoring systems, overhead line fittings, rigging hardware, and engineering steel or rebar-related components. This checklist helps buyers turn those source references into a quote-ready request without treating supplier claims as verified facts.

Core RFQ Checklist

Use the fields below for any industrial hardware request. Add project-specific requirements where applicable.

1. Product Identification

  • Product name or family
  • Drawing, BOM, catalog reference or sample
  • Matching components in the same system

2. Dimensions & Interfaces

  • Diameter, length and section dimensions
  • Thread, profile, hole or connection details
  • Critical tolerances when required

3. Material & Performance

  • Material or grade requirement
  • Mechanical-property requirement if specified
  • Load or safety requirement if specified
  • Applicable project or product standard

4. Finish & Environment

  • Finish or coating
  • Corrosion or exposure requirement
  • Indoor, outdoor, structural or other application context

5. Quantity & Packing

  • Quantity by SKU or system component
  • Carton, bundle, crate or pallet requirement
  • Marking and label requirement

6. Inspection & Documents

  • Dimensional inspection
  • Material or test documentation
  • Coating or finish documentation
  • Third-party inspection if required

Extra Questions by Product Family

The product family determines which missing fields are most likely to cause a wrong quote.

Precision Rolled Threaded Steel

Confirm bar diameter and length, thread/profile requirement, matching nut/connector/plate, material or grade, finish, quantity and inspection requirements.

Formwork & Temporary Works

Confirm the actual product identity, dimensions, mating parts, drawing or project reference, reusable or consumable context where relevant, quantity and packing.

Anchoring Systems

Confirm anchor type, dimensions, substrate or application context, material, finish, required standard, installation requirement and any safety-critical verification.

Overhead Line Hardware

Confirm exact fitting type, interface dimensions, material, coating, strength requirement, applicable utility/project standard, quantity and inspection documents.

Rigging & Lifting Hardware

Confirm exact product type, size, grade, finish, working requirement, standard and certification requirement. Do not infer WLL from a catalog image.

Engineering Steel & Rebar Components

Confirm steel or rebar product type, dimensions, material/grade, coating, project standard, matching accessories, quantity, packing and required testing.

What We Will Not Assume From a Catalog Page

Material and grade
A product image or title may identify a family but does not automatically verify exact material chemistry, grade or mechanical property.
Load performance and suitability
Load ratings, working load limits, structural suitability and substrate compatibility are safety-critical and require evidence appropriate to the product and project.
Certification and standards
Certification logos, standard references or supplier claims must be checked before they are used as order requirements or public product claims.
Commercial terms
Prices, MOQ, packing, documentation and lead time are confirmed after manual review of the RFQ rather than published as fixed website promises.
