How to Prepare an RFQ for Formwork Tie Rods & Related Accessories

Formwork tie rods, template pull rods and related temporary-works accessories can be described differently by suppliers and project documents. A structured RFQ reduces the risk of quoting the wrong rod system or incompatible accessories.

Minimum RFQ Information

Use the project drawing or BOM whenever available.

  • Drawing, BOM or project specification
  • Rod / tie system name used by the project
  • Diameter, length and thread details
  • Required material or grade
  • Required finish or corrosion requirement
  • Matching nut dimensions and quantity
  • Connector / coupler details if applicable
  • Plate dimensions, thickness and hole details if applicable
  • Quantity by item
  • Packing and identification requirements
  • Inspection, test or documentation requirements

Do Not Quote From Appearance Alone

Similar-looking rods and accessories may not be interchangeable.

CHS does not infer thread compatibility, material grade, allowable load, certification or project suitability from a catalog photo. These items must be supported by buyer requirements or reliable supplier / project documentation.

Before Asking for Price

Confirm the information that most often changes the quotation.

GeometryDiameter, length, thread system and mating-component dimensions.
Material & FinishRequired grade, coating or corrosion requirement where specified.
Project ControlsStandard, approval, inspection and documentation requirements.
