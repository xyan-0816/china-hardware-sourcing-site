What Buyers Should Send

For engineering hardware, a product name alone is often not enough.

  • Drawing, BOM, project specification or clear product reference
  • Product family and intended application context
  • Dimensions, thread details and mating components where applicable
  • Required material, grade, finish or corrosion requirement
  • Required standard, approval or certification if project-controlled
  • Quantity by item and packing requirements
  • Inspection or documentation requirements

Prices, MOQ, packing, documentation and lead time are confirmed after manual RFQ review.
