RFQ Information to Confirm

A system RFQ is more reliable when the bar and matching components are reviewed together.

1. Product & Drawing

  • Component type: bar, nut, connector, plate or complete set
  • Drawing, BOM or project reference
  • Required matching components

2. Dimensions & Interface

  • Bar diameter and length
  • Thread or profile requirement
  • Nut and connector matching requirement
  • Plate dimensions and hole details when applicable

3. Technical Requirements

  • Material or grade if specified
  • Mechanical-property requirement if specified
  • Finish or coating
  • Applicable standard or project specification

4. Commercial & QC Requirements

  • Quantity by component
  • Packing and marking
  • Required certificates or inspection reports
  • Sample or drawing approval requirement

Evidence Boundary

Why this page does not publish a fixed specification table.

What does the current source material support?
It supports identification of the threaded steel bar family and matching nut, connector and plate components. Several pages contain bilingual product titles and partial tables.
What remains unverified?
Exact values and units in the tables, material grades, mechanical properties, standards, coating details, load performance, certification and project applicability are not treated as verified until independently reviewed against the relevant source and buyer requirement.
Why is this useful for buyers?
It lets the RFQ start from a correctly grouped product system instead of a generic “threaded rod” request, while keeping technical claims tied to the buyer drawing and verified supplier evidence.
