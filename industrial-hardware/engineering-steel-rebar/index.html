Engineering steel & rebar components

Precision Rolled Threaded Steel Bar Systems

CHS can review RFQs for precision rolled threaded steel bar product families and related components such as matching nuts, connectors and plates. Current product-library evidence supports the product-family identity, but it does not independently verify specific grades, standards, load ratings, certifications, material properties or supplier performance claims. Those items must be confirmed from buyer specifications and supporting documentation before quotation.

System Components Buyers May Need to Confirm

A system RFQ is usually clearer when each component is listed separately.

Threaded Steel BarConfirm diameter, length, thread form / system, required material or grade, quantity and project specification.
Matching NutConfirm compatibility with the bar system, dimensions, material / grade requirement and quantity.
Connector / CouplerConfirm intended bar connection, dimensions, matching thread system and any drawing or project requirement.
PlateConfirm plate dimensions, hole details, thickness, material requirement and relationship to the bar / nut assembly.

RFQ Information to Provide

Exact terminology can vary between catalogs and projects, so drawings are strongly preferred when the product name alone is ambiguous.

  • Drawing, BOM or project specification
  • Bar diameter and required length
  • Thread system / geometry if specified
  • Required material or grade
  • Matching nut, connector and plate details
  • Required finish or corrosion requirement
  • Applicable standard or project requirement
  • Quantity by item
  • Packing and identification requirements
  • Required inspection or documentation

Prices, MOQ, packing, documentation and lead time are confirmed after manual RFQ review.

Evidence Boundary

CHS does not treat supplier catalog wording as verified engineering performance data.

For this product family, final specifications must be confirmed against the buyer's drawing, BOM, project standard, approved sample or RFQ. Load ratings, mechanical properties, certifications, compliance statements and application suitability are not assumed from product appearance or catalog naming.
